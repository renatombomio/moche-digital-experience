# Moche — Digital Experience

A modern digital experience and website redesign concept for **Moche**, a Peruvian restaurant in Amsterdam.

The project focuses on translating Moche's identity into a refined, immersive and editorial web experience, combining strong typography, motion, photography and clear navigation across desktop and 
mobile.

---

## Overview

This project is a custom website experience designed around Moche's Peruvian culinary identity and restaurant atmosphere.

The interface has been designed to feel:

- Editorial
- Premium
- Minimal
- Immersive
- Contemporary
- Strongly visual

The experience prioritizes storytelling and interaction rather than relying on a conventional restaurant website layout.

---

## Experience

The website is structured as a continuous digital journey through several key experiences:

### Hero

The opening experience establishes Moche's visual identity through large-scale typography, imagery and motion.

### Food Experience

A visual presentation of Moche's food offering, highlighting signature dishes and the relationship between Peruvian cuisine and the restaurant's identity.

### Pisco Experience

A dedicated visual experience around Moche's pisco culture, drinks and bar atmosphere.

### About Us

A storytelling section introducing Moche, its identity, people and approach to Peruvian cuisine.

### Reviews

A dedicated guest experience built around Moche's strong guest ratings and customer feedback.

The section includes:

- TheFork guest rating
- Food rating
- Service rating
- Atmosphere rating
- Featured guest reviews
- Interactive horizontal exploration
- TheFork review CTA

### Contact

A concise contact experience containing:

- Location
- Telephone
- Email
- Opening hours
- Dog-friendly information
- Card-only payment information
- Google Maps integration
- Instagram

### Navigation

The website includes a full-screen navigation experience designed as part of the visual identity rather than a conventional dropdown menu.

Navigation includes:

- Food
  - Lunch
  - Dinner
- Drinks
- About Us
- Contact
- Book a Table

The Drinks section is intentionally kept as a single destination until the final drinks structure is confirmed with the restaurant.
